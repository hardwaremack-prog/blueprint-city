# Blueprint City

A blueprint-styled city planning sim. Start in March 1900 with §10,000 and build a city from empty land.

![Blueprint City screenshot](<Blueprint City - screenshot.png>)

## What you can do

- Zone **residential**, **commercial** and **industrial** land and watch demand change
- Lay roads and power lines, and pick your power: coal, solar or nuclear
- Add parks, police and fire stations to keep crime and fires down
- Build special buildings: airport, naval base, stadium, church, marina, disco, drive-in and beach
- Cross water with a bridge or a ferry
- Set the tax rate and fund each department, and take out or repay loans
- Save, load or start a new city

## How to play

Pick a tool on the left, then click and drag on the map to place it. Right-click (or hold) to bulldoze quickly.

## Run it

Download `blueprint_city_10.html` and open it in your web browser. No install, no account, nothing to set up.
